Story

Carefree 25-year-old Prabh meets Aavraj, a practical, successful 55-year-old businessman. Prabh's mistakes and carefree nature challenge Aavraj's rigid personality, while secretary Rajdeep becomes the bridge between them. As Prabh learns practicality from Aavraj, Rajdeep discovers joy and emotional freedom through Prabh. With Rajdeep's help, Prabh creates a fictional world to heal the misunderstanding between Aavraj and his estranged daughter. Their reunion restores Aavraj's happiness and brings him emotionally closer to Prabh.
