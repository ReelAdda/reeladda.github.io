Story

Basant Kaur dreams of representing India in kabaddi, but her life changes after she is sexually assaulted and later betrayed again. Pregnant and traumatized, she survives on the streets until her former classmate Fateh takes her home and cares for her and her newborn child. As Basant recovers her memories, she decides to fight for justice against those who harmed her.
