Story

The Table: K-Chef 2026 brings together Korea's top chefs to compete for the title of the country's best chef. Mysterious world-renowned chef Chris K., also known as Kang Han, Song-yi, who is fighting to save her restaurant, perfectionist star chef Woo-jin, and chefs with stories of their own face off in a fierce culinary battle. More than a competition, they find comfort in food and one another.
