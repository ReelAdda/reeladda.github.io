Story

Two villages are bound by a rivalry that has outlived the men who started it. For one young man, the feud stops being history the day his uncle murders his grandparents and carries his mother away. Left with nothing but the land beneath his feet and the promise he makes on it, he sets out to answer blood with blood. To bring his mother home, he must first confront the man who took her, and the price the soil demands is heavier than he knows.
