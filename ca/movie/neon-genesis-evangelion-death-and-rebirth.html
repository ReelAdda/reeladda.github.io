What the audience says

Neon Genesis Evangelion: Death and Rebirth is a solid, watchable Japanese film that mostly delivers on what it promises. It's sitting at 7.4/10 on TMDB across 520 ratings, which puts it comfortably above average.

Story

Originally a collection of clips from the Neon Genesis Evangelion TV series, Death was created as a precursor to the re-worked ending of the series. Rebirth was intended as that re-worked ending, but after production overruns Rebirth became only the first half of the first part of The End of Evangelion, with some minor differences.
