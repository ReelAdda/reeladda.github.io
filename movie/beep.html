Story

BeeP is a mind-bending crime-fantasy about Ilandevan, a young man who can step inside photographs and uncover the thoughts hidden behind them. But when a life-changing event leaves his extraordinary ability useless, he must find another way to uncover the truth while two seemingly unrelated worlds of college dreams, drugs and murder collide.
