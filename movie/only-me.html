Story

A disoriented man wakes up in a car in the middle of nowhere, with a gun, a cryptic note and a bound woman. As events unfold, he is drawn into the disturbing world of a Mumbai model whose carefully constructed rituals blur the lines between victim and predator. Through violence, deception and haunting memories, the film explores trauma, control and the struggle to reclaim one's agency.
