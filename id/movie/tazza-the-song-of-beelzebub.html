Story

Jang Taeyoung is an instinctive moneymaker with uncanny luck and charm. Rising from the depths, Jang returns to a high-stakes global gambling arena. Waiting for him is Park, now at the top of a vast international operation. Entangled with a Yakuza-backed executive, the two Taeyoung's rivalry escalates into an inevitable endgame, where every hand could cost everything.
