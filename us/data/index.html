An estimate: the cinema date plus that studio's median above. No studio has announced these dates.

Method. The window runs from a film's cinema release in the United States to its digital release there, both from TMDB's release dates. A digital release is usually the first day a film can be bought or rented at home, which comes before it reaches a subscription service. The studio is the producing studio TMDB lists first among the major groups; a film's distributor can differ. The dates are entered by TMDB's contributors, so the coverage line says how many films have one. Films whose digital date comes before their cinema date, or more than a year after it, are left out. A studio gets a row once it has 8 films with both dates.
