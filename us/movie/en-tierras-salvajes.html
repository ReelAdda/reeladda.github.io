Story

En tierras salvajes is a Mexican telenovela produced by Salvador Mejía Alejandre for Televisa and originally broadcast on Las Estrellas from July 31, 2017, to February 25, 2018. The story follows Isabel Montero, a young woman caught in a love triangle with brothers Daniel and Aníbal Otero. As rivalry, family conflicts, and hidden secrets come to light, their lives become intertwined in a struggle between love, ambition, betrayal, and redemption.

Director: Ramón Campos
