Story

Violetta is a lonely but exceptionally talented teenager who returns to her hometown of Buenos Aires after living many years on the “old continent”. Back home, she will visit Studio 21, a space full of art and music, and will become a frequent visitor. In her new life, she finds true friends and love, as she discovers her innate talent and passion for music and, in turn, builds and faces the great experience of growing up.
