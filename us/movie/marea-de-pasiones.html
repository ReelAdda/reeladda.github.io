Story

Marea de Pasiones is a Mexican telenovela produced by Giselle González for TelevisaUnivision and originally broadcast on Las Estrellas from March 18 to September 1, 2024. It is based on the Portuguese telenovela Paixão (2017), created by Inês Gomes. Adapted by Juan Carlos Alcalá, with direction by Alba Gil and Eric Morales, the telenovela stars Oka Giner, Matías Novoa, Margarita Muñoz, Alejandro de la Madrid, Luz María Jerez, Blanca Guerra, Ana Belena, and Sergio Reynoso.
