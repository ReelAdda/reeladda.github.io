Story

After an accident that sidelined her, Luna returns to her beloved Jam & Roller. Putting on her skates again won't be easy, especially when a hidden villain's plan starts to complicate her life. She'll also face a dilemma, torn between old flames. Amidst songs, adventures, and reunions with friends, Luna will have to overcome many obstacles to fulfill her dream.
