How long does a film take to reach streaming?

Updated 14 November 2026 · two sources, kept apart: FilmyChill's own daily checks in 18 countries, and TMDB's digital release dates for India
Which number to quote
  • 49 days: the median of 161 moves from cinema to streaming that FilmyChill saw itself since 27 August 2026: 90 films in 18 countries, each film counted once for every country it reached. This is the headline figure on this page.
  • 50 days: the median for 751 Indian-language films, from TMDB's digital release dates, March 2022 to October 2026. The longer record for India.
  • 37 days: India from FilmyChill's own checks, 63 films since October 2026. An early reading, mostly Tamil and Telugu films so far.

Method. FilmyChill checks streaming availability every day in 18 countries and records the first date each title appears with a provider. Only films that opened in cinemas in that country count: the window runs from that country's cinema release (TMDB's theatrical date for it) to our first sighting on a streaming service. TV series and straight-to-streaming films are left out, and so are films that were already streaming when we started watching a country, because we never saw them arrive. Gaps over two years are excluded. Groups too small for their own row are counted in each total, and the line under each table says how many. Films still in cinemas join the figures when they reach streaming, so while the archive is young the medians lean towards faster releases. This is a record of when a film became visible to us, which is normally the day it drops but is not a studio announcement.

India. Measured figures cover every Indian-language film TMDB lists as opening in Indian cinemas, from October 2026; earlier, only films on our own lists were followed. A film already streaming when we began following it is left out. The TMDB figures use a different source: the digital release date TMDB's contributors record for India, which for Indian films is normally the streaming premiere. It reaches back further, but it is volunteer-entered, so we publish its coverage beside it and never mix it with our own measurements. Films whose digital date comes before their cinema date, or more than a year after it, are left out. The rows behind it are in this CSV.

Correction, 4 October 2026. Earlier versions of this page also counted TV series, straight-to-streaming films and titles we hadn't seen arrive, which pulled every median down to a few days.
