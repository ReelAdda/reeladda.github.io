Story

A series of mysterious abductions by colorful aliens has been spreading fear across the globe. Amid the chaos, Aoi continues to live a peaceful life with her boyfriend, Yuzu. But one night, while Aoi is asleep, Yuzu is abducted by the colorful aliens. As anti-alien sentiment intensifies across Earth, Aoi begins rigorous training, driven by her desire for revenge. Then one day, deep in the forest, she encounters a different kind of alien...
