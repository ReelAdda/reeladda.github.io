Story

College student Miwa Inuzuka has always longed for romance, yet she has never had a relationship that truly worked out. After starting university, she meets the bright and outgoing Saeko Sawatari and despite their completely opposite personalities, the two unexpectedly discover that they both like women. Can two girls who are both bad at love find their own happiness?
