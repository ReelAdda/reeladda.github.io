Privacy

Short version: FilmyChill shows no ads and never sells your data. We count visits with Google Analytics, with its advertising features switched off and without cookies for visitors in Europe and the UK. The site also runs Grow, a widget from Mediavine for saving films and getting recommendations, which sets its own cookies and offers an optional email sign-up. Both are explained below. The only personal data FilmyChill itself handles is a vote you choose to cast, tied to a random ID, not to your name, email or account.

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

Who runs FilmyChill

FilmyChill (filmychill.com) is an independent site run by Vikram Sharma, India. It is responsible for the data described on this page.

What happens when you just browse

Visit statistics. We measure visits with Google Analytics, a service of Google. It records things like the pages viewed, how you arrived (the referring site or search), your browser, device type and screen size, and your approximate location (country and city, worked out from your connection), and interactions such as scrolling, clicks on links to other sites, searches on the site and trailer plays. On the homepage it also records which section and position a film was opened from, for example “the third card in Streaming”, so we can improve the lists. We use it only to understand which pages and lists are useful.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics runs without cookies: it sends anonymous counts that can't recognise you on a later visit.
  • Everywhere else, it sets first-party cookies (named _ga) so a returning browser is counted once, kept for up to two years.
  • Advertising features are switched off everywhere: no advertising cookies, no ad personalisation, no Google signals, and nothing from Google Analytics is used to show you ads.

Google processes this data under its privacy policy. You can stop it with your browser's privacy settings or tracking protection, or with Google's opt-out browser add-on.

Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers may log technical details of each request (such as the IP address) for security and reliability; that is handled under GitHub's privacy statement, and FilmyChill does not receive or use those logs.

Images and trailers. Film posters load from TMDB's image servers (image.tmdb.org). Trailers are embedded with YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com) and only load when you play one; once you do, YouTube's own policies apply.

Grow: saving films and recommendations

FilmyChill uses Grow, a service run by Mediavine, Inc., for its save-for-later and recommendation features. Its script loads on every page, once the page itself has finished loading. When it runs, Grow:

  • sets cookies and stores data in your browser, to remember you and what you've saved;
  • records which pages you view and what you do in the widget, to recommend other pages and to measure how the widget is used;
  • offers an optional sign-up with your email address, to keep your saved films across devices and to send you updates if you ask for them.

Grow works out from your connection whether privacy laws such as the EU's GDPR apply where you are, and adjusts what it does. This data is handled by Mediavine under its own policies, set out in the Mediavine legal and privacy center. Its recommendations are worked out by Recombee, the recommendation service Grow uses. FilmyChill does not load the advertising-identity services Grow can use (EUID and UID2), or the Google web font it asks for. To remove what Grow stored, clear this site's cookies and data in your browser; if you signed up, ask Mediavine to delete your account through the link above.

What stays in your own browser

Some features remember things on your device only, using your browser's local storage (not cookies): your My List, the language filter you picked, films you've already seen marked as new, and which way you voted if you vote. Nothing here is sent to us. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

If you vote “Worth it” or “Not worth it”

Film pages ask “Watched it? Was it worth it?”. Only if you tap an answer:

  • your browser creates a random anonymous ID (no name, email, phone or sign-up) using Google Firebase Authentication;
  • one small record is saved in Google Cloud Firestore: that ID, the film, your answer, which FilmyChill country page you were on, and the time;
  • the ID is kept in your browser so you can change your vote. Each browser gets one vote per film.

Why: to count how many people found each film worth watching, so visitors' verdicts can become part of the FilmyChill Score. Votes are only ever used as totals per film; we never look at, sell or share individual votes. Who handles it: Google (Firebase) stores the records on our behalf, in Google's Mumbai (India) data centre; as with any online service, Google may process technical data such as your IP address to provide it, under Firebase's privacy terms. How long: vote records are kept while the film's score is in use; totals may be kept indefinitely.

Removing it: clear this site's data in your browser. That deletes the anonymous ID, after which your past votes can no longer be linked to you or your device by anyone, including us.

What we don't do

  • No advertising cookies. The only cookies on the site are Google Analytics' (outside Europe and the UK) and Grow's, both described above.
  • No ads, and FilmyChill never sells your data.
  • No FilmyChill accounts. The only place to enter an email is Grow's optional sign-up, and it goes to Grow.
  • No profiling by FilmyChill. Grow uses the pages you view to recommend others, as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU's GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data and to complain to a data protection authority. Because FilmyChill holds no name, email or account for anyone, the practical way to exercise these rights is the one above: clearing this site's data in your browser removes everything that could connect a vote to you. For anything Grow holds, including an account you created there, contact Mediavine through its privacy center. For any privacy question, email filmychill.contact@gmail.com.

Children

FilmyChill is a general-audience site and does not knowingly collect data from children. Voting is optional and anonymous.

Changes

If what the site collects ever changes, this page changes first, with a new “last updated” date.
