Story

In a coastal village forgotten by time, young Juca approaches Zé Cangulo, a fisherman living in isolation on his boat. A silent bond forms between them, shaped by waiting and the sea. By bringing to light a submerged past in which the boy recognizes himself, this encounter opens up the possibility of a fresh start.
