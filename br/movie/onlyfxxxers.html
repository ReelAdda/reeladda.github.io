Story

An adult content creator rents an isolated house with a small group of collaborators to shoot a Halloween special and expand her online reach. Between erotic photo sessions, staged scares and livestreams, an antique music box awakens a demonic force that turns performance into real and increasingly grotesque violence. As the cameras keep rolling and the audience grows, the desire to be seen exacts a brutal price. A gore-horror fable about the commodification and trivialization of intimacy in the age of algorithms, where exposure and even suffering becomes engagement.
