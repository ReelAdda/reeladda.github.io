Story

An Argentine dramatic television miniseries co-produced by TNT and FLOW. It follows the life of a nun who, after fifteen years, manages to escape from a cloistered convent where she was a victim of psychological abuse and held against her will. Starring Carolina Kopelioff, Lorena Vega, Rita Cortese, and Julieta Zylberberg.

Creator: Paula Hernández
