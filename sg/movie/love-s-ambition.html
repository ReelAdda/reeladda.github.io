Story

Renowned anchor Xu Yan and urban elite Shen Haoming appear to be the perfect couple but struggle to fit into each other’s social circles due to their differing backgrounds. When conflict arises, Xu Yan leaves, prompting Shen Haoming to realize his deep love for her and attempt to win her back. On their journey, they face challenges, shed their facades, and rediscover their true selves.
