Story

An operation that shook the nation starting on February 11, 2013. At that time, Sulu terrorists infiltrated Kampung Tanduo in Lahad Datu, Sabah. By the end of "Op Daulat", 56 terrorists and six civilians were reported killed. The conflict also saw 12 national heroes fall in battle after sacrificing their lives to defend the nation's sovereignty.
