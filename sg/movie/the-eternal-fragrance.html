Story

When the Sea Calamity strikes and the Divine Tree appears, whoever claims its fruit gains power over all creation, sparking a bloody war among the immortals, demons, and monsters. A century later, Lei Xiuyuan seeks out Qingqiu orphan Xiaobangchui to save his brother. Fate brings them to Chufeng Academy, where loyal friends give her warmth and love begins to bloom. As their bond deepens from friendship to something more, the truth of her origins slowly unravels, threatening everything they've built.
