Story

Anbu, a taxi driver from Kannagi Nagar, meets Regina, a mute IT employee, during his regular trips and falls for her. When he learns about her troubled personal life, he suppresses his feelings. One night, Regina dies in what appears to be an accident—but soon, it’s revealed to be a planned murder. Shockingly, multiple unconnected people are involved. RAGE follows Anbu’s journey to uncover the truth behind Regina’s death and the tangled motives that led to it.
