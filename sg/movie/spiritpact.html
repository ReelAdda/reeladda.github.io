Story

Heir of an old famous family of exorcist, Yang Jinghua(You Keika in Japanese) is stricken by poverty after losing his parents. He struggles as a street fortune teller and computer recovery part timer to earn money. One night in a junkyard, he meets the mysterious yet charismatic exorcist, Duanmu Xi(Tanmoku Ki in Japanese) , fighting an evil spirit. This encounter is the beginning of a fated story for Yang Jinghua. Unfortunately for him, he was actually hit by a car and became a ghost.
