Story

King, Queen, Jack, and Ace-four misfits surviving through petty crimes in Chennai-found their world turned upside down when COVID-19 lockdowns shut down public transport, their main source of income. Desperate for a way out, King discovered an audio recording about a bungalow filled with black money, supposedly guarded by a lone watchman. Seeing it as their golden opportunity, the gang set out to execute the perfect heist. However, upon entering the bungalow, the door slammed shut behind them, and a chilling voice welcomed them.
