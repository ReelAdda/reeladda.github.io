Story

In an era of economic despair and record-high unemployment, where becoming a YouTuber is the ultimate dream job, the popular YouTube crew 'Killing Time' collapses overnight after an unforeseen accident claims a team member's life during a live broadcast. Desperate for a successful comeback, the members plot to return with far more extreme and sensational content. However, just as success seems within reach, mysterious figures appear and propose the 'real' Killing Time.
