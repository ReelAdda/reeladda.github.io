Story

Hong Eun Jo leads a double life: a servant and healer by day and a Robin-Hood-like thief named Gil Dong by night. Grand Prince Do Wol, brother to the King, begins to investigate the case. An encounter with a monk leads to them swapping souls, and they are forced to navigate each other's complex worlds.
