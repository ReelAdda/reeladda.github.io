Story

PERFECT STORM: THE MOVIE depicts the process of five young men with different backstories facing unexpected dangers. Notably, writer Jeon Hyo-jin of the representative Korean mystery variety show 'Crime Scene' series participated in screenwriting also participated in TXT's Hello Strangers. The five members appear as characters with their own stories, confronting incidents that shake their daily lives and dangers akin to a massive storm.
