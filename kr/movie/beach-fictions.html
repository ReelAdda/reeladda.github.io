Story

Tae-ju, tormented by whispers and rumors swirling around her for reasons she doesn’t understand, boards a train as if fleeing. On the train, she happens to meet novelist Ewha, who tells Tae-ju she resembles the protagonist of a novel she’s writing. Arriving at the beach, Tae-ju meets a street magician named Manhyuk, visits a fortune-teller recommended by Ehwa, and spends some time with complete strangers at a guesthouse.
