Story

Growing closer as they prepare for the group discussion, they dare to dream that all six might make it — until the day before the interview, when the company announces its verdict: only one will be hired. On the day of the interview, mysterious envelopes arrive, each containing one of their secrets — and suspicion begins to take hold. Who among the six is the liar?
