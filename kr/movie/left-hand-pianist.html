Story

While pursuing his doctoral studies in the United States, promising pianist Lee Hoon suffers a stroke that paralyzes his right hand. Returning to Korea, he discovers the possibility of performing with his left hand alone and begins rebuilding both his life and identity through music. Supported by his mother and inspired by the joy that first drew him to the piano, he overcomes his limitations and embraces a new path.
