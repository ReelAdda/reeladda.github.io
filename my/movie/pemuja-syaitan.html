Story

Just months after finishing school, former classmates, Khatijah, Ana, Marissa, Nazim, P and Julie reunite in their coastal hometown. Tagging along are Halim, P's cousin and Amira, Nazim's Current Conquest. The night takes a dark turn when they dig up a time capsule they buried sometime ago. Tension begins to rise. unspoken betrayals, fractured trust, and painful memories resurface, as the night unleash a terror unseen and unknown. The morning after, Inspector Zul is dispatched to the island, and witnesses something he hasn't seen before.
