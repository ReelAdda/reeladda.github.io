Story

Haiku is a heartfelt romantic drama about second chances and healing. Mano, once an immature and possessive college boy, loses his first love Kodi, a bold and caring girl from a traditional Coimbatore family, after his insecurities and long-distance struggles ruin their relationship. Years later, Mano, now more mature, meets Nivi, an independent, lower-middle-class girl raised by a single mother, who is recovering from a toxic relationship with Arul. Mano helps Nivi rediscover what true love means, while Nivi gives Mano a chance to love without control and without fear. But when Nivi learns about Mano’s toxic past with Kodi, doubts and insecurities threaten their bond. HAIKU beautifully explores how first love may break us, but second love teaches us to heal, forgive, and love better.

Director: Yuvaraj Chinnasamy
