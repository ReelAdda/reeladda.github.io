Story

"Cocaine" is a gripping crime thriller that delves deep into the murky intersection of law enforcement and criminality. Set against the vibrant yet perilous backdrop of Mumbai, the story revolves around a powerful drug cartel that wields significant control over the cocaine trade with the connivance of corrupt police officials and politicians. Abhimanyu Singh stars as Jalal, a diligent police inspector who finds himself entangled in the cartel's sinister web.
