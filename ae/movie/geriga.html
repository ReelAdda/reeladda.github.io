Story

In the remote village of Naivad, Kerala, villagers follow GERIGA, a sacred ritual to summon the dead. Desperate for viral fame, Vijay and his four YouTuber friends stage the ritual as a hoax, but horror strikes when Vijay is possessed just as news breaks of his father's sudden death. Now, the group must fight supernatural forces and finish the ritual in twelve days to free him before time runs out.
