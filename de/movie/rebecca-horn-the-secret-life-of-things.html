Story

This film explores the complex body of work by Rebecca Horn, whose kinetic objects, installations, and films expanded the concept of art in the 20th century. Her work paved the way for future generations of artists. Director Claudia Müller explores Horn’s work within the context of her time and examines its significance in the present day.
